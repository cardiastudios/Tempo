How It Works

Simple, fast, beautiful

01

Choose a design

Browse our exclusive templates and pick the one that speaks to your love story. Each design has an ID: A for Best Sellers, B for Book Invitations, and C for Classic Invitations.

02

Send your details

Share your template ID, names, date, venue, schedule, and any personal touches you'd like included.

03

We customize it

We adapt the template to your wedding: names, date, venue, schedule, photos, wording, sections, and small design details are adjusted to match your event.

04

Share with guests

Send a single link to all your guests. RSVPs go straight to your inbox automatically, with a live Google Sheet that updates in real time.
